About This Map

Covers: Alameda, Contra Costa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara and Santa Cruz Counties.

Source: Each city's, town's and county's own sandbag page or notice, plus county-wide lists from San Mateo County, Santa Cruz County and Valley Water, the ALCO VOAD flooding resources list, 211 Alameda County and Contra Costa County Public Works. Checked November 10–18, 2026.

Before You Go:

  • Most sites serve only residents of that city, or of unincorporated county areas
  • Supplies run out during storms and most sites are unstaffed
  • Call the number in the pop-up to confirm before driving over
  • Sites marked "call to confirm" do not say who can use them; they are shown as open to all
  • Red sandbags are one-day events, not permanent sites; they disappear from the map after their date

Using Sandbags:

  • Fill bags only 1/2 to 2/3 full so they lie flat; a full bag weighs 30–50 lbs, so work in pairs and wear gloves
  • Lay them in rows like bricks, overlapping each row by half a bag, and press each one down to close gaps
  • Keep walls low (2–3 bags high) and put plastic sheeting behind them if you have it
  • Point water toward a drain or the street, never onto a neighbor's property, and don't block storm drains

When You're Done:

  • Clean, dry bags can be stored in a shaded, covered spot and reused next storm
  • Clean sand can go in your garden or yard; empty bags go in the trash
  • If a bag touched floodwater, don't reuse it or put the sand in a garden; ask your city how to dispose of it
